Tableau K-Means clustering Infographic

Household Spending Patterns in West Java, 2023

Data Visualization course (STA1262) · Group project, 5 members

Goal: Compare how households in West Java's districts and cities spend their money, and group the regions by spending pattern.

Data and method: SUSENAS 2023 household survey data, grouped into 5 spending categories with K-Means clustering (silhouette score 0.2872), then presented in an interactive Tableau dashboard and an infographic poster.

Key finding: Higher-spending groups put more of their budget into non-food items, from 44% in the Very Low group to 85% in the Very High group. Inequality also differs by region: Kota Bogor has the highest Gini ratio (0.54) and Kabupaten Cianjur the lowest (0.33).

Goal: Show how monthly sales changed from 2012 to 2015 and which countries contribute the most profit.

R

Mapping Malaria Prevalence in Ethiopia

Goal: Map where school-based malaria surveys took place and how falciparum prevalence varies between sites.

Method: Spatial data in R with sf, ggplot2 and ggspatial, plus interactive leaflet maps with a regional boundary shapefile, custom color palettes and an elevation raster.

Key finding: Most survey sites show very low prevalence, while a few sites stand out with clearly higher rates.

R

Exploring Three Datasets with ggplot2

Goal: Turn three public datasets into clear charts: diabetes indicators by age, average income by province and education level, and COVID-19 in Indonesia. Group assignment, 3 members.

Method: ggplot2 with smoothing curves, tile maps, columns and points, styled with custom themes.

Key finding: The COVID-19 chart shows two clear waves of positive cases from March 2020 to June 2021, with recoveries tracking new cases and deaths staying comparatively low.
